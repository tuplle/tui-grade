# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in `tui-grade` to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about code and behavior, not about people.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a change for scope, safety or maintenance reasons.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish student data, credentials, private reports or someone else’s personal information.
- Remember that this project runs untrusted code; do not encourage unsafe testing outside a disposable environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam or bad-faith reports.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled as confidentially as possible. Do not open a public issue with personal details.

Maintainers may warn, remove content, close threads, or temporarily or permanently ban participation. They will explain
a decision when it is safe and appropriate to do so.
